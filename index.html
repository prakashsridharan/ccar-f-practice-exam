How it works

  1. LearnOne short lesson per exam subtopic, with the traps the exam uses as wrong answers, check questions and a hands-on exercise. Search box on every page: type a term such as PreToolUse or RAG to jump straight to the section that explains it.
  2. PracticeTimed practice tests in the exam's domain proportions. Every answer explains why it is right and why the others are wrong.
  3. Check readinessScores by domain, a readiness estimate and a retry list of the questions you missed. Progress is saved as you go, and follows you to any device if you sign in.
  4. ReviseA cheat sheet per exam: the decision rules, key facts and common distractors on three printed pages or fewer, each point linked to its lesson.

Frequently asked questions

Which certifications are covered?

Claude (Anthropic) certifications today: Claude Certified Associate - Foundations (CCAO-F), Claude Certified Architect - Foundations (CCAR-F), Claude Certified Architect - Professional (CCAR-P, in versions 1.1 and 1.0) and Claude Certified Developer - Professional (CCDV-P), each with a learning guide, a cheat sheet and practice tests. AWS practice exams are in preparation, and more exams are added as their official guides are published.

Is it really free?

Yes. Every learning guide, cheat sheet and practice test on this site is free, and none of it needs an account.

Do I need to sign in?

No. Everything works without an account, and your progress is saved in your browser. Signing in is optional and free: use Google, or your email with a one-time link and code (there is no password). Your practice test scores, answers and completed lessons are then saved to your account, so you can start on your laptop and carry on from your phone or another browser. Signing out clears them from that browser, and signing back in brings them back. See the privacy policy for what is stored.

Are these real exam questions?

No. Every lesson and question is original, written from the public exam guides and the providers' documentation. Live exam content is confidential, and nothing here comes from a real exam.

Is this affiliated with the certification providers?

No. This is an independent study aid. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Anthropic, AWS or any other certification provider.

What happens when an exam guide changes?

Each guide follows the published exam guide task by task. When a new version is published, the lessons are compared against it and updated, and where the exam guide and the current documentation differ, a note in the lesson explains both.

Important Note

Every question is original, written from the public exam guides and their blueprint objectives. None comes from a real exam or recreates a question seen in a test sitting, since live exam content is confidential. A high score here does not guarantee a pass: pair it with hands-on work and the official documentation. This is an independent study aid, not affiliated with or endorsed by Anthropic.
