How it works

  1. LearnOne short lesson per exam subtopic, with the traps the exam uses as wrong answers, check questions and a hands-on exercise.
  2. PracticeTimed practice tests in the exam's domain proportions. Every answer explains why it is right and why the others are wrong.
  3. Check readinessScores by domain, a readiness estimate and a retry list of the questions you missed. Progress stays in your browser.
  4. ReviseA cheat sheet per exam: the decision rules, key facts and common distractors on three printed pages or fewer, each point linked to its lesson.

Frequently asked questions

Which certifications are covered?

Claude (Anthropic) certifications today: Claude Certified Architect - Foundations (CCAR-F) and Professional (CCAR-P), each with a learning guide, a cheat sheet and practice tests. AWS practice exams are in preparation, and more exams are added as their official guides are published.

Is it really free?

Yes. Every learning guide, cheat sheet and practice test on this site is free, with no sign-up. Your progress is saved in your browser, and you can download a backup to move it to another device.

Are these real exam questions?

No. Every lesson and question is original, written from the public exam guides and the providers' documentation. Live exam content is confidential, and nothing here comes from a real exam.

Is this affiliated with the certification providers?

No. This is an independent study aid. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Anthropic, AWS or any other certification provider.

What happens when an exam guide changes?

Each guide follows the published exam guide task by task. When a new version is published, the lessons are compared against it and updated, and where the exam guide and the current documentation differ, a note in the lesson explains both.

Important Note

Every question is original, written from the public exam guides and their blueprint objectives. None comes from a real exam or recreates a question seen in a test sitting, since live exam content is confidential. A high score here does not guarantee a pass: pair it with hands-on work and the official documentation. This is an independent study aid, not affiliated with or endorsed by Anthropic.
