Privacy Policy

Last updated 5 November 2026

CertPrep is a free study site for certification exams. You can use all of it without an account. This page explains what we collect, why, where it is kept, and how to remove it. Questions: contact@pratechlabs.com.

Without an account

  • Your progress stays in your browser. Practice test scores, study answers and completed lessons are saved in your browser's local storage. They never leave your device unless you sign in. Clearing your browser data removes them.
  • Visit counts. When a page opens we record the page, your browser type (user agent), the site that linked to us and any campaign tags in the link (such as utm_source). We do not record your name or email, and we do not use this to identify you. It tells us which pages and which shared posts are useful.
  • Feedback. If you send feedback with the Feedback button, we store your message, the rating and topic you choose, the address and title of the page you sent it from and your browser type. Your email is stored only if you add it, and is used only to reply to you. Only the site owner can read feedback.
  • No advertising or tracking cookies. We do not set cookies.

With an account

Signing in is optional. It keeps your progress in step across your devices.

  • What we store: your email address; if you sign in with Google, the name and profile picture address Google shares; the dates you signed up and last signed in; and your study progress (practice test attempts and scores, your latest answer to each question, study mode answers, completed lessons and exercise ticks).
  • What we do not store: passwords (there are none), payment details, or your Google contacts, files or any other Google data.
  • How it is used: to sign you in and to show your progress on any device. In future it may power features for you, such as a progress dashboard or study suggestions. We will not sell it, share it for advertising, or send you marketing email without asking first.
  • Signing out removes your progress from that browser. It stays in your account and comes back when you sign in again.
  • Sign-in emails: when you ask for a sign-in link, we send one email with a link and a 6-digit code.

Services we use

  • Supabase stores accounts, saved progress, visit counts and feedback, and runs sign-in.
  • Resend sends the sign-in emails.
  • Google handles "Continue with Google" if you choose it, and serves the fonts on these pages.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the site. Like any web host, it keeps standard server logs.

These providers may process data in countries other than yours. Each acts under its own privacy terms.

How long we keep it

Account data and saved progress are kept until you delete your account. Visit counts are kept as aggregate statistics. Feedback is kept while it is useful for improving the site, and you can ask for yours to be deleted.

Your choices

  • Delete your account at any time: sign in, open the account menu in the header and choose "Delete my account". This deletes your account and the progress saved to it straight away. Progress stored in your browser stays there until you clear it.
  • See or correct your data, or ask any question: email contact@pratechlabs.com.

Children

CertPrep is meant for adults preparing for professional certifications. It is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly create accounts for them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.

Important Note

CertPrep is an independent study aid, not affiliated with or endorsed by Anthropic or any other certification provider.
