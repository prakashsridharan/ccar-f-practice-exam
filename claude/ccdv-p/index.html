CCDV-P Practice Exam

Claude Certified Developer - Professional (Version 1.0)

A free practice exam for the Claude Certified Developer - Professional (CCDV-P) certification, following Anthropic's exam guide 1.0 (November 2026). 140 original scenario-based questions in the exam's format: about half are multiple response, each with a rationale, the exam skill it tests, and a reference link to the relevant Anthropic documentation. No sign-up and nothing to install.

About the CCDV-P exam

The CCDV-P certification validates that you can operate Claude-powered applications, agents and workflows in production at scale. The real exam is 70 scenario-based items (60 scored and 10 unscored, not identified) in 120 minutes, proctored online or at a test center, with criterion-referenced scaled scoring and a pass mark of 720 on a scale of 100 to 1,000.

Exam domains

  • D1 - Advanced Agent Architecture and Orchestration (17%): multi-agent orchestration, durable execution and state, architecture selection from production data, and context and memory policies for long-running agents.
  • D2 - Production Integration, Reliability, and Knowledge Grounding (20%): retries, circuit breakers and fallbacks, throughput and multi-tenant quotas, production MCP and change management, and retrieval and grounding at scale.
  • D3 - Observability and Production Monitoring (12%): traces, token, cost and cache metrics under privacy constraints, SLIs, SLOs and alerting, and trace analysis at scale.
  • D4 - Advanced Debugging and Incident Response (10%): isolating failures among model, harness, tools and data, including refusals and grounding failures, and incident response with learning loops.
  • D5 - Evaluation Engineering and Quality Assurance (12%): golden sets, regression suites and CI gates, calibrated LLM judges, and online quality measurement.
  • D6 - Cost and Latency Optimization at Scale (8%): cost models, routing and cascades, caching strategy, batch economics, tenant budgets, time to first token, streaming, parallel tools and effort.
  • D7 - Runtime Guardrails and Production Security (11%): layered guardrails and approval gates, prompt injection and exfiltration defenses, red teaming, workload identity, secrets and audit.
  • D8 - Agent Fleet Operations for Software Delivery (10%): parallel agents on worktrees, AI code review and verification, unattended and event-driven agents, and organization-wide Claude Code configuration.

Two tests, two modes

The 140 questions form two practice tests, each a fixed set of 70 questions in the same proportions as the official blueprint, with no question shared between tests. Choose a test, then a mode:

  • Exam mode: exam conditions, with a 120-minute timer, flag-for-review and a score report broken down by domain. Your best and latest scores are kept in your browser (or in your free account if you sign in, so they follow you to any device), and an unfinished test can be resumed. Random Mix draws a new set of 70 from the whole bank.
  • Study mode: self-paced, showing the correct answer, the rationale and a documentation link after each question, with a filter by domain.

Learning guide

New to a topic? The free CCDV-P Learning Guide has 25 lessons, one for each skill in the exam guide, each with check questions and a hands-on exercise set in a realistic case study. Answer panels in the practice tests link back to the lesson for each question's skill.

CCDV-P exam FAQ

What is the exam format?

The CCDV-P exam (exam guide 1.0, November 2026) has 70 scenario-based items in 120 minutes. 60 are scored and 10 are unscored trial items, which are not identified, so treat every item as scored. Items are multiple choice or multiple response, and each item states how many responses to select. It is proctored, online or at a test center, and booked through Pearson VUE.

What score do I need to pass?

A scaled score of 720 on a scale of 100 to 1,000. Anthropic does not publish how raw answers convert to the scaled score, so a percentage on this site is a guide, not a prediction. Aim for 80% or more overall and 75% or more in every domain.

How much does the exam cost?

The exam guide lists the CCDV-P exam at $175 USD, and you pay when you register through the Anthropic Partner Academy and Pearson VUE. The price shown at checkout reflects any partner discount.

Who can take the exam?

Claude certification exams are open to people who work at organizations in the Claude Partner Network. You register through the Anthropic Partner Academy, and on exam day you need a valid government-issued photo ID whose name matches your registration exactly.

Is there a prerequisite?

No. The Claude Certified Developer - Foundations certification is recommended but not required, and the guide treats its scope as assumed knowledge. The credential is awarded on exam performance alone.

How long is the certification valid?

12 months from the date it is awarded. Renewing on time is a free, unproctored assessment on the Anthropic Partner Academy; if the credential lapses, you take the full exam again at the full fee.

Can I retake the exam?

Yes, after a waiting period: 14 days after a first failed attempt, 30 days after a second and 90 days after a third, with at most four attempts in a rolling 12 months. Each attempt is paid separately.

How close are these practice tests to the real exam?

They follow the official blueprint: 70 items, 120 minutes and the same domain proportions across all eight domains, with about half the items multiple response (marked Select TWO or Select THREE). Every question is original and written from the public exam guide, so none of them comes from the real exam.

Do I need an account?

No. Everything here is free and works without signing in, and your progress is saved in your browser. Sign in only if you want to continue on another device.

Important Note

Every question in this app is original, written from the publicly available exam guide and the skills in its blueprint. Nothing here comes from the real exam, and no question recreates one seen in a test sitting: live exam content is confidential, and every candidate agrees to keep it that way. A high score here is not a guarantee of passing, so pair this practice with hands-on work operating Claude systems and Anthropic's official documentation.
