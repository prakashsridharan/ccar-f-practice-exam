Which Claude certification should I take?

All 5 current Claude certifications side by side: who each one is for, what it tests, and where to start.

Anthropic's certifications follow three tracks: Associate for people who use Claude at work, Developer for people who build with it, and Architect for people who design Claude solutions. Pick the track that matches your work, then start at Foundations.

I use Claude at work

For people who use Claude at work: consultants, sellers, analysts and team leads who pick the right use cases and features, check the output and handle data responsibly.

CCAO-F

I build with Claude

For engineers who build with the Claude API, Claude Code and MCP, from a first integration to agents running in production.

CCDV-F → CCDV-P

I design Claude solutions

For architects who design Claude solutions end to end: agent architectures, tools and context, evaluation, cost, governance and safety.

CCAR-F → CCAR-P

None of the exams has a prerequisite; Foundations first is the usual order.

Questions

Which Claude certification should I take first?

Start from what you do with Claude. If you use it at work without building on it, take Associate - Foundations (CCAO-F). If you build with the API, the Agent SDK or Claude Code, take Developer - Foundations (CCDV-F), then Developer - Professional (CCDV-P) once you run Claude systems in production. If you design solutions and make architecture decisions, take Architect - Foundations (CCAR-F), then Architect - Professional (CCAR-P).

What is the difference between the Developer and Architect certifications?

Developer exams test how you build and operate: writing the API calls, agents, tools and prompts, then running them reliably, cheaply and safely in production. Architect exams test design decisions: which architecture fits the problem, the trade-offs between options, and, at Professional level, evaluation, governance, risk and communication with stakeholders. Many topics overlap, so the two tracks share ground such as agents, tools, MCP and Claude Code.

Do I need a Foundations certification before a Professional exam?

No. None of the exams has a prerequisite. The CCAR-P exam guide treats the Architect Foundations scope as assumed knowledge, and the CCDV-P guide recommends Developer Foundations (CCDV-F) as preparation, so Foundations first is the usual order.

Who can take the exams?

Claude certification exams are open to people who work at organizations in the Claude Partner Network. You register through the Anthropic Partner Academy, and on exam day you need a valid government-issued photo ID whose name matches your registration exactly.

What changes on 7 December 2026?

Anthropic's exam guide 1.1 takes effect for Associate Foundations, Developer Foundations and Architect Professional. This site already follows guide 1.1 for all three. CCAR-P Version 1.0 stays on the site until that date for people who sit the exam before it.

Are the guides and practice tests free?

Yes. Every learning guide, cheat sheet and the two practice tests for each exam are free, with no sign-in needed. Signing in only saves your progress across devices.
