    How to read a CCAO-F question

    Most items describe a business professional using claude.ai and something that went wrong or a choice to make. Six habits pick the right answer:

    1. Find the gap before the fix. Generic output means missing context; wrong numbers mean prose arithmetic; old facts mean stale knowledge or memory.
    2. Ask what happens if it is wrong. Consequence, reversibility, audience and regulation set the review depth.
    3. Verify independently. A source, a recomputation or an expert, never Claude vouching for itself.
    4. Put each thing in its slot. Rules in instructions, facts in knowledge, procedures in Skills, continuity in memory.
    5. Keep accountable decisions with people. Claude drafts; the accountable person decides and sends.
    6. Classify data before it moves. Approved path first, then minimize; incognito does not make data safe.

    Domain 3 · 15%Capability, Model, and Context Selection

    Remember: choose by need: recurring context, current facts, exact numbers, a real file, a shared procedure, live data, continuity.

    Recurring work, same background and formatProject 3.1A new chat each time
    Last 60 days, many sources, citedResearch 3.1Built-in knowledge
    A quick current factWeb search 3.1Research

    Data in Drive, Gmail, CalendarA connector (your permissions) 3.1Research
    Structured, high volume, easy to fixFast tier, low effort 3.2Top tier "to be safe"
    Ambiguous and high stakesMost capable tier, higher effort 3.2Fast tier

    Incognito chats are still retained under retention policy and are only available outside Projects. Chats in a Project share its instructions and knowledge, not each other's threads.
