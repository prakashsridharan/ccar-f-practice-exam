    Case study · fictional company

    Ironwood Logistics

    A Kansas City freight and warehousing company whose operations, procurement and project teams use Claude for weekly reports, vendor reviews and capacity planning.

    The business

    Ironwood Logistics, based in Kansas City, Missouri, runs 11 warehouses and a regional trucking network, with 1,200 employees. Its operations, procurement and project management teams use Claude on an Enterprise plan.

    How the teams use Claude

    WorkWhoToday
    Weekly operations report (on-time shipments, pick errors, overtime)Six site managersEach pastes spreadsheet extracts into a new chat and writes their own prompt
    Quarterly carrier performance reviewProcurementClaude summarizes contracts and scorecards; analysts compute on-time rates by hand
    Warehouse expansion studyStrategy and PMOMix of chats, uploaded reports and web browsing
    Vendor emails and contract renewalsProcurementDrafted with Claude; sent by buyers

    Measured baseline

    • The six weekly reports use six different formats; the regional director spends 90 minutes reconciling them.
    • A carrier was nearly dropped last quarter after an on-time rate was miscalculated in a chat (91% reported, 96% actual).
    • The weekly report data lives in Google Sheets on a shared drive, updated every Monday morning.

    Policy and constraints

    • Customer shipment data may be used in the Enterprise workspace; it must not leave it.
    • Payments to carriers and contract renewals are approved by the procurement manager.
    • Figures in any report that goes to the leadership team must be computed, not estimated.

    People

    Procurement Manager, Luis OrtegaFair, accurate carrier decisions
    PMO Lead, Grace KimA defensible expansion recommendation
    Site Manager (Joplin), Tom BeckerLess time on reports, more on the floor
