    CCAO-F/Learning guide/Case studies

    Case Studies

    Four fictional US organizations used by the exercises at the end of every lesson. Each one already uses Claude in day-to-day business work, with the facts a practitioner would have on hand, so every exercise can be done without access to a real organization.

    How the cases work

    Every lesson ends with an Apply it exercise set in one of these organizations. Each uses claude.ai for everyday knowledge work: writing, analysis, research and recurring reports. The exercises ask the questions an Associate faces at work: how to specify the request, whether the output is fit to use, which capability and model to choose, what to delegate, how to configure a Project, and how to handle sensitive data.

    The organizations, people and numbers are invented for study. Any resemblance to a real organization is coincidental.

    CaseSectorHow it uses Claude
    Brightwater CommunicationsMarketing agencyCampaign drafts, client reports and research for 14 client accounts
    Ironwood LogisticsFreight and warehousingOperations reports, vendor reviews and planning for 11 warehouses
    Fairhaven Unified School DistrictK-12 educationParent communications, lesson materials and HR work across 22 schools
    Copperline Credit UnionFinancial servicesMember communications, policy questions and compliance reviews

    Assumptions used in every case

    • Each organization uses a Team or Enterprise plan of claude.ai with Projects, Artifacts, Research, code execution and file creation, Skills, and the Google Workspace connectors. Admins decide which connectors and Skills are enabled.
    • Each organization has a written AI policy that says which data may be used in Claude and where. The relevant rules are listed in each case.
    • Model names are written as tiers (fast, balanced, most capable), because the exam tests the judgment, not the current product names.
    Important Note

    These lessons are original study notes, written from the publicly available exam guide and Anthropic's public help center and documentation. They contain no real exam content: live exam content is confidential, and every candidate agrees to keep it that way. This is an independent study aid and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Anthropic. Reading the lessons is not a guarantee of passing, so pair them with the practice tests and regular hands-on use of Claude in your own work.
