    Case study · fictional company

    Copperline Credit Union

    An Omaha credit union whose member services, HR and compliance teams use Claude for member communications, policy answers and regulatory reviews, under strict rules for member financial data.

    The business

    Copperline Credit Union, based in Omaha, Nebraska, serves 140,000 members from 18 branches, with 520 employees. It is regulated and examined regularly.

    How the teams use Claude

    WorkWhoToday
    Replies to member complaints and questionsMember servicesAgents draft with Claude and send after review
    Staff questions about HR policyHRA Project with the policy manual, set up 14 months ago
    Summaries of new regulations for the boardComplianceClaude chats, sometimes without the regulation text attached
    Marketing for a new savings productMarketingCopy drafted with Claude

    Measured baseline

    • Member services handles about 6,000 written contacts a month.
    • The HR Project recently quoted last year's 401(k) match and named a benefits manager who has left.
    • An internal audit found reviews of member replies were skipped on 12% of busy days.

    Policy and constraints

    • Member account numbers, Social Security numbers and card numbers must never be entered into Claude. Other member information may be used only in the Enterprise workspace, with memory off for member work.
    • Any communication that tells a member about a fee, a decision on their account or a complaint outcome is reviewed by an agent before sending.
    • Credit and lending decisions are made by staff, never by Claude.
    • The policy does not yet say anything about using Claude for translation or for marketing claims.

    Chief Compliance Officer, Rachel AdamsNo regulatory surprises
    Member Services Manager, Omar HaddadFast, accurate replies
