    CCAO-F Learning Guide

    Claude Certified Associate - Foundations (exam guide 1.1)

    Twenty-one short lessons, one for each skill in the CCAO-F exam guide 1.1 (effective 7 December 2026), grouped by the six exam domains. Each lesson explains the judgment the skill asks you to make when you use Claude at work, the traps that catch candidates, and where to read more in Anthropic's help center and documentation.

    Written and maintained by Prakash Sridharan, Claude Certified Architect

    lessons complete
    Before the examCheat sheetThe whole Associate Foundations guide on three printed pages or fewer. For each domain, one line to remember and an "if the question says this, pick that" table, plus the facts worth memorising. Read it the day before the exam.
    Domain 115% of the exam

    Task Definition and Prompting

    Turning a business objective into a well-specified request: role, context, task, constraints and output format; breaking large deliverables into staged steps with checkpoints; and diagnosing an underperforming prompt to the specification gap that caused it.

    3 lessons
    Domain 220% of the exam

    Output Evaluation and Quality Control

    Judging Claude output before it is used: checking it against requirements, sources and professional standards, catching fabricated citations and confident invented specifics, setting review depth by consequence and audience, and adapting the output and its format for the reader.

    4 lessons
    Domain 315% of the exam

    Capability, Model, and Context Selection

    Choosing what to use for a task: chat, Projects, Artifacts, research, code-executed analysis, Skills, connectors and memory; the model tier and effort level that fit its quality, speed and cost needs; and when to restart, summarize, persist to a Project or rely on memory.

    3 lessons
    Domain 418% of the exam

    Workflow Design and Integration

    Using Claude across a whole deliverable, from requirements to cited research, computed analysis and an actionable plan; assigning each workflow step as AI-executed, human-reviewed or human-retained; building repeatable workflows that others can own; and explaining what Claude can and cannot do to stakeholders.

    4 lessons
    Domain 515% of the exam

    Configuration and Knowledge Management

    Setting Claude up for recurring work: Project instructions, knowledge, Skills and scoped memory; connectors such as Google Drive and Gmail and what to upload instead; persistent instructions that keep output consistent; and tracing a configuration whose output has drifted to the layer responsible.

    4 lessons
    Domain 617% of the exam

    Responsible Use, Data Protection, and Governance

    Deciding whether a use case is appropriate, appropriate with safeguards or inappropriate; protecting sensitive and regulated data through classification, redaction and memory and incognito controls; and closing compliance, ethics and Diligence gaps against organizational AI policy.

    3 lessons

    Every lesson ends with a short exercise set in one of four fictional companies. Their facts are on the case studies page.

    Not on the exam

    The exam guide says items assess judgment in work scenarios. You do not need to memorize:

    • Prices, plan allowances, context-window sizes or rate limits.
    • How to build with the Claude API, agents or tools; that is the Developer and Architect track.
    • Exact menu paths in claude.ai, which change between releases. Know what each feature is for and when to use it.

    Ready to test yourself? The CCAO-F practice tests cover the same six domains, with a rationale and documentation link for every question.

    Important Note

    These lessons are original study notes, written from the publicly available exam guide and Anthropic's public help center and documentation. They contain no real exam content: live exam content is confidential, and every candidate agrees to keep it that way. This is an independent study aid and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Anthropic. Reading the lessons is not a guarantee of passing, so pair them with the practice tests and regular hands-on use of Claude in your own work.
