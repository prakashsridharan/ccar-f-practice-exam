    CCAR-P Learning Guide

    Claude Certified Architect - Professional (exam guide 1.1)

    Thirty-nine short lessons, one for each skill in the CCAR-P exam guide 1.1, which applies from 7 December 2026, grouped by the seven exam domains. Each lesson explains the decisions the skill asks you to make, the traps that catch candidates, and where to read more in Anthropic's documentation.

    Written and maintained by Prakash Sridharan, Claude Certified Architect

    lessons complete
    Before the examCheat sheetThe whole Professional guide 1.1 on three printed pages or fewer. For each domain, one line to remember, the tables worth memorising, and "if the question says this, pick that" tables. Read it the day before the exam.
    Domain 118% of the exam

    Solution Design & Architecture

    Deciding what Claude does and what people keep, judging whether an architecture holds under load and trust limits, choosing patterns and orchestration, tying options to business value, and picking the deployment surface and agent runtime.

    6 lessons
    Domain 213% of the exam

    Claude Models, Prompting & Context Engineering

    Choosing the model tier that meets every binding constraint, diagnosing and revising system prompts, matching the prompting technique to the failure, and managing tokens, cache breakpoints, reasoning effort and long-running context.

    6 lessons
    Domain 321% of the exam

    Integration

    Connecting Claude to enterprise systems safely and reliably: least-privilege tools, authentication posture, the binding performance constraint, monitoring gaps, retrieval architecture, connection mechanisms, progressive discovery and production reliability controls.

    8 lessons
    Domain 417% of the exam

    Evaluation, Testing & Optimization

    Proving a system works and keeps working: finding evaluation gaps, deriving evaluations from the business process, correcting LLM-judge bias, designing fair comparisons, isolating root causes, cutting cost and latency, and migrating model versions.

    7 lessons
    Domain 516% of the exam

    Governance, Safety & Risk Management

    Keeping systems safe and compliant: placing guardrails on the misuse path, closing prompt-injection routes through documents, tools, MCP servers and Skills, ranking risks, gating irreversible actions, evidencing compliance and tracing bias to its source.

    6 lessons
    Domain 67% of the exam

    Stakeholder Communication & Lifecycle Management

    Spotting commitments in requirements or SLAs that the architecture cannot meet, writing decision records that make trade-offs and residual risk explicit, and handing a system to the team that will run it.

    3 lessons
    Domain 78% of the exam

    Developer Productivity & Operational Enablement

    Scaling Claude Code across an organization: packaging workflows as plugins with managed settings, configuring the Agent SDK and Claude Code permission modes, hooks and headless runs, and keeping humans accountable for AI-generated code.

    3 lessons

    Every lesson ends with a short exercise set in one of four fictional companies. Their facts are on the case studies page.

    Ready to test yourself? The CCAR-P practice tests cover the same seven domains, with a rationale and documentation link for every question.

    Important Note

    These lessons are original study notes, written from the publicly available exam guide and Anthropic's public documentation. They contain no real exam content: live exam content is confidential, and every candidate agrees to keep it that way. This is an independent study aid and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Anthropic. Reading the lessons is not a guarantee of passing, so pair them with the practice tests and hands-on work with Claude.
