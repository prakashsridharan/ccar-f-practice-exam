CCAR-P Practice Exam

Claude Certified Architect - Professional (Version 1.1)

A free practice exam for the Claude Certified Architect - Professional (CCAR-P) certification, updated for Anthropic's exam guide 1.1, which applies from 7 December 2026. 140 original scenario-based questions in the exam's format: about half are multiple response, and each test also has Yes/No statement grids and matching items, each with a rationale, the exam objective it tests, and a reference link to the relevant Anthropic documentation. No sign-up and nothing to install.

About the CCAR-P exam

The CCAR-P certification validates that you can design, build and deliver production-grade AI solutions on Anthropic's Claude platform. From 7 December 2026 the real exam is 70 scenario-based items (60 scored and 10 unscored, not identified) in 120 minutes, proctored online or at a test centre, with criterion-referenced scaled scoring and a pass mark of 720 out of 1000.

Exam domains

  • D1 - Solution Design & Architecture (18%): delegating work between Claude and people, evaluating architectures, patterns including Skills, orchestrator and subagent decomposition, business value, and choosing a deployment surface and agent runtime (including Claude Managed Agents).
  • D2 - Claude Models, Prompting & Context Engineering (13%): model tiers, diagnosing system prompts, prompting techniques and native thinking, token economics, cache breakpoints, reasoning effort and the context lifecycle.
  • D3 - Integration (21%): least-privilege tools, authentication posture, the binding performance constraint, monitoring gaps, retrieval architecture, MCP versus API/CLI versus agent handoff, progressive discovery, and reliability controls such as idempotency and fallback routing.
  • D4 - Evaluation, Testing & Optimization (17%): evaluation coverage gaps, deriving evaluations from the business, LLM-judge bias, experimental design, root-cause isolation from traces, cost and latency, and model version migration.
  • D5 - Governance, Safety & Risk Management (16%): guardrail placement, prompt injection through documents, tools, MCP servers and Skills, risk ranking, human-in-the-loop gates, compliance controls and sources of bias.
  • D6 - Stakeholder Communication & Lifecycle Management (7%): unsupportable commitments in requirements and SLAs, architecture decision records, and operational handoff.
  • D7 - Developer Productivity & Operational Enablement (8%): packaging Claude Code workflows as plugins, configuring permission modes, hooks and headless runs, and accountability for AI-generated code.

Two tests, two modes

The 140 questions form two practice tests, each a fixed set of 70 questions in the same proportions as the official blueprint, with no question shared between tests. Choose a test, then a mode:

  • Exam mode: exam conditions, with a 120-minute timer, flag-for-review and a score report broken down by domain. Your best and latest scores are kept in your browser (or in your free account if you sign in, so they follow you to any device), and an unfinished test can be resumed. Random Mix draws a new set of 70 from the whole bank.
  • Study mode: self-paced, showing the correct answer, the rationale and a documentation link after each question, with a filter by domain.

CCAR-P exam FAQ

What is the exam format?

From 7 December 2026 (exam guide 1.1) the CCAR-P exam has 70 scenario-based items in 120 minutes. 60 are scored and 10 are unscored trial items, which are not marked, so treat every item as scored. Candidates report that about half the items are multiple response, a few are Yes/No statement grids or matching items, and the rest are single choice. Each item states what to select. It is proctored, online or at a test centre, and booked through Pearson VUE.

What score do I need to pass?

A scaled score of 720 out of 1000. Questions do not all carry the same weight and Anthropic does not publish the conversion, so a percentage on this site is a guide, not a prediction. Aim for 80% or more overall and 75% or more in every domain.

How much does the exam cost?

Anthropic lists this exam at $175 USD on its certifications page (the Foundations exam is $125 USD), and you pay there when you register. Check that page for the current price.

Who can take the exam?

Claude certification exams are open to people who work at organizations in the Claude Partner Network. You register with a company email on your organization's partner domain, and on exam day you need to be 18 or older with a government-issued ID.

How long is the certification valid?

Anthropic states that the credential is valid for 12 months. Renewing on time is a free, unproctored assessment on the Anthropic Partner Academy; if the credential lapses, you take the full exam again.

Can I retake the exam?

Yes, after a waiting period: 14 days after a first failed attempt, 30 days after a second and 90 days after a third, with at most four attempts in a rolling 12 months. Each attempt is paid separately.

What changed in exam guide 1.1?

The exam grew from 63 to 70 items, the domain weights changed (Integration and Governance up, Stakeholder Communication down to 7%), each domain now lists skills tested through scenarios, and the Foundations scope is assumed knowledge. New topics include choosing a deployment surface and agent runtime, Claude Managed Agents, idempotent tools, prompt injection through MCP servers and third-party Skills, bias in model judges, model version migration, and Claude Code plugins and permission modes.

Should I take Foundations first?

It is not required, but exam guide 1.1 treats the Foundations scope as assumed knowledge. If the Foundations topics are new to you, the CCAR-F guide on this site covers them.

How close are these practice tests to the real exam?

They follow the official blueprint: the same number of items, the same time limit and the same domain proportions, with the item types candidates report: about half multiple response (marked Select N), plus Yes/No statement grids and matching items. Every question is original and written from the public exam guide, so none of them comes from the real exam.

Do I need an account?

No. Everything here is free and works without signing in, and your progress is saved in your browser. Sign in only if you want to continue on another device.

Important Note

Every question in this app is original, written from the publicly available exam guide and the objectives in its blueprint. Nothing here comes from the real exam, and no question recreates one seen in a test sitting: live exam content is confidential, and every candidate agrees to keep it that way. A high score here is not a guarantee of passing, so pair this practice with hands-on work with Claude and Anthropic's official documentation.
