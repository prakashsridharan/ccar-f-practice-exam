CCAR-P Practice Exam

Claude Certified Architect - Professional

A free practice exam for the Claude Certified Architect - Professional (CCAR-P) certification. 126 original scenario-based questions, including multiple-response items, each with a rationale, the exam objective it tests, and a reference link to the relevant Anthropic documentation. No sign-up and nothing to install.

About the CCAR-P exam

The CCAR-P certification validates that you can design, build and deliver production-grade AI solutions on Anthropic's Claude platform. The real exam is 63 multiple choice and multiple response items in 120 minutes, proctored online or at a test centre, with criterion-referenced scaled scoring and a pass mark of 720 out of 1000.

Exam domains

  • D1 - Solution Design & Architecture (17%): translating business problems into Claude solutions, workflow versus agentic patterns, multi-agent orchestration and business value.
  • D2 - Claude Models, Prompting & Context Engineering (13%): model selection, system prompts and guardrails, few-shot and chain-of-thought, prompt caching, compaction and Skills.
  • D3 - Integration (19%): least-privilege tools, authentication and authorization, observability, RAG pipeline design, and choosing between MCP, API/CLI and cloud routes.
  • D4 - Evaluation, Testing & Optimization (16%): metrics, golden datasets, LLM-as-judge, A/B and shadow testing, diagnosing failures, and latency and cost optimization.
  • D5 - Governance, Safety & Risk Management (14%): guardrails, prompt-injection defence, human-in-the-loop placement, GDPR, HIPAA and FedRAMP, and fairness.
  • D6 - Stakeholder Communication & Lifecycle Management (14%): discovery, communicating trade-offs, SLAs and feedback loops, and architecture documentation.
  • D7 - Developer Productivity & Operational Enablement (7%): configuring Claude Code for teams, rolling out AI tooling, and supporting debugging and operations.

Two tests, two modes

The 126 questions form two practice tests, each a fixed set of 63 questions in the same proportions as the official blueprint, with no question shared between tests. Choose a test, then a mode:

  • Exam mode: exam conditions, with a 120-minute timer, flag-for-review and a score report broken down by domain. Your best and latest scores are kept in your browser, and an unfinished test can be resumed. Random Mix draws a new set of 63 from the whole bank.
  • Study mode: self-paced, showing the correct answer, the rationale and a documentation link after each question, with a filter by domain.
