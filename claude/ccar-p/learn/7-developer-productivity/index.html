Developer Productivity & Operational Enablement

Enabling engineering teams: configuring Claude Code with CLAUDE.md, Skills, hooks, subagents and permission modes, rolling out AI tooling well, and supporting debugging and operations.

  1. 7.1Configuring Claude Tools for TeamsSet teams up with a shared, enforced Claude Code configuration, distribute Skills through the mechanism that fits each audience, bound spending up front, and roll out through champions rather than a single big launch.
  2. 7.2Improving Developer WorkflowsFit AI assistance into the existing development workflow at the stages where it helps, give Claude ways to verify its own work, and keep a review discipline that ensures people still understand what ships.
  3. 7.3Debugging and Operational SupportSupport teams running Claude systems by connecting symptoms to architectural causes, and leave behind runbooks and escalation paths so the team can resolve the familiar problems without you.
