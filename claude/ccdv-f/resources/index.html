Important Note

These lessons are original study notes, written from the publicly available exam guide and Anthropic's public documentation. They contain no real exam content: live exam content is confidential, and every candidate agrees to keep it that way. This is an independent study aid and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Anthropic. Reading the lessons is not a guarantee of passing, so pair them with the practice tests and hands-on work building with Claude.
