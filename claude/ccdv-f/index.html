CCDV-F Practice Exam

Claude Certified Developer - Foundations (Version 1.1)

A free practice exam for the Claude Certified Developer - Foundations (CCDV-F) certification, following Anthropic's exam guide 1.1 (effective 7 December 2026). 140 original scenario-based questions in the exam's format, some asking for two or three responses, each with a rationale, the exam skill it tests, and a link to the relevant Anthropic documentation. No sign-up needed; sign in only if you want your progress on every device.

About the CCDV-F exam

The CCDV-F certification validates that you can build and ship applications, agents and workflows with Claude: integrating the Messages API and SDKs, building agents with the Claude Agent SDK, using Claude Code, engineering prompts and context, building tools and MCP servers, choosing models and managing cost, evaluating and debugging, and applying secure-by-design practice. The real exam is 70 scenario-based items (60 scored and 10 unscored, not identified) in 120 minutes, proctored online or at a test center, with criterion-referenced scaled scoring from 100 to 1,000 and a pass mark of 720.

Exam domains

  • D1 - Agents and Workflows (17%): architecture and runtime selection, Agent SDK construction and hooks, and agent pattern diagnosis.
  • D2 - Applications and Integration (20%): Messages API mechanics, application boundary and surface behavior, configuration and SDLC integration, and integration resilience.
  • D3 - Claude Code (8%): operating Claude Code, and customizing and extending it for a team.
  • D4 - Eval, Testing, and Debugging (10%): evaluation design and failure-mode diagnosis.
  • D5 - Model Selection and Optimization (12%): generation mechanics and model options, model tier selection and release planning, and cost diagnosis and optimization.
  • D6 - Prompt and Context Engineering (15%): context management for long-running sessions, prompt revision, structured output and defensive parsing, and grounding and source citation.
  • D7 - Security and Safety (8%): prompt injection and jailbreak defense, deterministic guardrails and least privilege, and API key and credential handling.
  • D8 - Tools and MCP (10%): tool design and response shaping, MCP server development and integration, and capability mechanism selection.

Two tests, two modes

The 140 questions form two practice tests, each a fixed set of 70 questions in the same proportions as the official blueprint, with no question shared between tests. Choose a test, then a mode:

  • Exam mode: exam conditions, with a 120-minute timer, flag-for-review and a score report broken down by domain. Your best and latest scores are kept in your browser (or in your free account if you sign in, so they follow you to any device), and an unfinished test can be resumed. Random Mix draws a new set of 70 from the whole bank.
  • Study mode: self-paced, showing the correct answer, the rationale and a documentation link after each question, with a filter by domain.

Learning guide

New to a topic? The free CCDV-F Learning Guide has 24 lessons, one for each skill in the exam guide, each with check questions and a hands-on exercise set in a realistic case study. Answer panels in the practice tests link back to the lesson for each question's skill.

CCDV-F exam FAQ

What is the exam format?

The CCDV-F exam (exam guide 1.1, effective 7 December 2026) has 70 scenario-based items in 120 minutes. 60 are scored and 10 are unscored trial items, which are not identified, so treat every item as scored. Items are multiple choice, and each item states how many responses to select. It is proctored, online or at a test center, and booked through Pearson VUE.

What score do I need to pass?

A scaled score of 720 on a scale of 100 to 1,000. Anthropic does not publish how raw answers convert to the scaled score, so a percentage on this site is a guide, not a prediction. Aim for 80% or more overall and 75% or more in every domain.

How much does the exam cost?

The exam guide lists the CCDV-F exam at $125 USD, and you pay when you register through the Anthropic Partner Academy and Pearson VUE. The price shown at checkout reflects any partner discount.

Who is the exam for?

Hands-on developers who build with Claude: software, AI/ML, full-stack and backend engineers and solutions engineers, typically with one to five years of experience and at least six months of work with Claude. It tests building agents and workflows, integrating the Messages API, using Claude Code, prompt and context engineering, tools and MCP, model selection, evaluation and secure-by-design practice.

Who can take the exam?

Claude certification exams are open to people who work at organizations in the Claude Partner Network. You register through the Anthropic Partner Academy, and on exam day you need a valid government-issued photo ID whose name matches your registration exactly.

Is there a prerequisite?

No. Hands-on experience building with Claude is recommended preparation but not required, and the credential is awarded on exam performance alone.

How long is the certification valid?

12 months from the date it is awarded. Renewing on time is a free, unproctored assessment on the Anthropic Partner Academy; if the credential lapses, you take the full exam again at the full fee.

Can I retake the exam?

Yes, after a waiting period: 14 days after a first failed attempt, 30 days after a second and 90 days after a third, with at most four attempts in a rolling 12 months. Each attempt is paid separately.

How close are these practice tests to the real exam?

They follow the official blueprint: 70 items, 120 minutes and the same domain proportions across all eight domains, with some items asking for two or three responses (marked Select TWO or Select THREE). Every question is original and written from the public exam guide, so none of them comes from the real exam.

Do I need an account?

No. Everything here is free and works without signing in, and your progress is saved in your browser. Sign in only if you want to continue on another device.

Important Note

Every question in this app is original, written from the publicly available exam guide and the skills in its blueprint. Nothing here comes from the real exam, and no question recreates one seen in a test sitting: live exam content is confidential, and every candidate agrees to keep it that way. A high score here is not a guarantee of passing, so pair this practice with hands-on work building with Claude and Anthropic's official documentation.
