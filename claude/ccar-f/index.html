CCAR-F Practice Exam

Claude Certified Architect - Foundations

A free practice exam for the Claude Certified Architect - Foundations (CCAR-F) certification. 120 original scenario-based questions, each with a detailed rationale, an explanation of why the other options are wrong, and a reference link to the relevant Anthropic documentation. No sign-up and nothing to install.

About the CCAR-F exam

The CCAR-F certification validates your ability to design, build and optimize AI-powered applications using Claude and the Anthropic platform. The real exam is 60 multiple-choice and multiple-response questions in 120 minutes, proctored online or at a test centre, and uses scaled scoring with a pass mark of 720 out of 1000.

Exam domains

  • D1 - Agentic Architecture & Orchestration (27%): the agent loop, stop_reason handling, task decomposition, subagent delegation and multi-agent coordination.
  • D2 - Tool Design & MCP Integration (18%): tool schemas, precise descriptions, actionable error messages, right-sized tool catalogs and exposing tools over MCP.
  • D3 - Claude Code Configuration & Workflows (20%): CLAUDE.md memory, custom slash commands, hooks, permission scoping, sessions and CI/CD integration.
  • D4 - Prompt Engineering & Structured Output (20%): JSON schemas via tool_use, required versus optional fields, validation and repair loops, and batching for cost.
  • D5 - Context Management & Reliability (15%): context window budgeting, compaction, preserving citations through summarization and recovering state after failure.

Two modes

Practice Tests 1 and 2 are fixed sets of 60 questions each, in close to the official blueprint proportions, with no question shared between them. Choose a test, then a mode:

  • Exam mode: exam conditions, with a 120-minute timer, flag-for-review and a score report broken down by domain. Your best and latest scores are kept in your browser (or in your free account if you sign in, so they follow you to any device), and an unfinished test can be resumed. Random Mix draws a new set of 60 from all 120 questions.
  • Study mode: self-paced, showing the correct answer, the rationale and a documentation link after each question, with a filter by domain.

Learning guide

New to a topic? The free CCAR-F Learning Guide has 30 lessons, one for each task in the exam guide, each with short code examples, check questions and a hands-on exercise set in one of the exam's six scenarios. Answer panels in the practice tests link back to the lesson for each question's objective.

CCAR-F exam FAQ

What is the exam format?

The CCAR-F exam has 60 items in 120 minutes. Items are multiple choice or multiple response, and each one states how many answers to select. It is proctored, online or at a test centre, and booked through Pearson VUE.

What score do I need to pass?

A scaled score of 720 out of 1000. Questions do not all carry the same weight and Anthropic does not publish the conversion, so a percentage on this site is a guide, not a prediction. Aim for 80% or more overall and 75% or more in every domain.

How much does the exam cost?

Anthropic lists this exam at $125 USD on its certifications page (the Professional exam is $175 USD), and you pay there when you register. Check that page for the current price.

Who can take the exam?

Claude certification exams are open to people who work at organizations in the Claude Partner Network. You register with a company email on your organization's partner domain, and on exam day you need to be 18 or older with a government-issued ID.

How long is the certification valid?

Anthropic states that the credential is valid for 12 months.

Should I take Foundations or Professional first?

Anthropic recommends starting at Foundations and moving on to Professional. This exam covers the core building blocks of Claude applications; the Professional exam assumes them and goes further.

How close are these practice tests to the real exam?

They follow the official blueprint: the same number of items, the same time limit and the same domain proportions, with multiple-response items marked Select N. Every question is original and written from the public exam guide, so none of them comes from the real exam.

Do I need an account?

No. Everything here is free and works without signing in, and your progress is saved in your browser. Sign in only if you want to continue on another device.

Important Note

Every question in this app is original, written from the publicly available exam guide and the objectives in its blueprint. Nothing here comes from the real exam, and no question recreates one seen in a test sitting: live exam content is confidential, and every candidate agrees to keep it that way. A high score here is not a guarantee of passing, so pair this practice with hands-on work with Claude and Anthropic's official documentation.
